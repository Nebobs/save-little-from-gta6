The Mission

A 5-phase plan to
level up for real.

Every great game has a walkthrough. Here's yours for life.

01

Understand the Root Cause

Before intervening, figure out why Little plays so much. Gaming fills a void — the question is, what void?

  • Have an honest, no-judgment conversation
  • Ask what GTA gives him that real life doesn't
  • Identify if it's escapism, boredom, or social
02

Set Soft Screen Time Limits

Don't ban the game — that creates resistance. Agree on boundaries together so he has buy-in.

  • Max 2hrs weekdays, 3hrs weekends
  • No playing after midnight
  • Use Cold Turkey or Windows Family Safety
03

Replace the Dopamine Source

GTA gives Little achievement, excitement, and progress. Recreate that rush in the real world.

  • Gym — physical progress he can see weekly
  • Learn to code, design, or make music
  • Start a side hustle or build something real
04

Build a Future He's Excited About

Gaming fills a vacuum. The real fix is giving Little something to work toward.

  • Map out his 3-year life vision together
  • Identify one small step for this week
  • Make his goal feel more exciting than the game
05

Be a Good Friend, Not a Cop

The intervention only works if Little doesn't feel attacked. Make real life more attractive than GTA6.

  • Never shame him for playing
  • Invite him to do things, don't tell him to stop
  • Celebrate every small win out loud
  • Check in weekly — with genuine care
